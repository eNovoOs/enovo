About Us

Built by Operators,
for Operators

eNovo was born from a simple frustration: business owners were paying for a dozen tools that didn't talk to each other and came with zero guidance. We built the system we wished existed.

Our Mission

Simplify Growth

We believe scaling a business shouldn't require a computer science degree or a $2,000/month tech stack. Every entrepreneur deserves access to enterprise-grade tools paired with the training to actually use them.

That's why eNovo combines a complete business operating system with hands-on education, coaching, and a community of growth-minded operators — all under one roof.

One Platform. Zero Chaos.

Software, training, implementation, and support — everything you need to grow, nothing you don't.

Our Core Values

Relentless Improvement

Deliver on the Promise

Our Journey

The eNovo Story

The Beginning

Frustration Sparked an Idea

Our founders were running their own businesses and spending more time managing disconnected tools than actually growing. They knew there had to be a better way — so they built it.

Building the Platform

From Vision to Reality

We spent months combining CRM, funnels, email, SMS, booking, automation, and analytics into a single, intuitive platform. But we quickly realized software alone wasn't enough.

The Academy

Training Changes Everything

We launched the eNovo Academy — live webinars, step-by-step courses, done-for-you templates, and coaching. Clients who used both the software and training saw dramatically better results.

Today

Trusted by 1,200+ Businesses

Today, eNovo serves entrepreneurs, coaches, agencies, and service providers worldwide. We continue to ship updates weekly, add new training monthly, and obsess over making growth simpler for everyone.
