Liste de vérification d'audit commercial

A comprehensive 25-point audit to identify gaps in your marketing, sales, and follow-up systems. Score yourself and find your biggest opportunities.

Marketing (Score each 1-5)

  • Do you have a clear, documented ideal customer profile?
  • Is your website converting visitors into leads consistently?
  • Do you have at least 2 active lead generation channels?
  • Are you tracking cost per lead and cost per acquisition?
  • Do you have a content strategy with a publishing schedule?

Sales (Score each 1-5)

  • Do leads receive a response within 5 minutes?
  • Is there a documented sales process with clear stages?
  • Do you track close rates and average deal value?
  • Are proposals sent within 24 hours of a discovery call?
  • Do you have a system for handling objections?

Follow-Up & Retention (Score each 1-5)

  • Do lost leads get an automated nurture sequence?
  • Is there a re-engagement campaign for inactive contacts?
  • Do clients receive a structured onboarding experience?
  • Are you collecting reviews and testimonials systematically?
  • Do you have a referral program or upsell strategy?
Scoring: 60-75 = Strong foundation. 40-59 = Significant gaps to address. Below 40 = Major overhaul needed. Focus on the lowest-scoring areas first for the biggest impact.

Landing Page Swipe File

High-converting landing page structures and copy frameworks used by 8-figure businesses. Plug in your offer and launch fast.

High-Converting Landing Page Structure

  • Hero Section: Clear headline stating the dream outcome. Subheadline addressing the pain. Single CTA button above the fold.
  • Problem Agitation: 3-5 pain points your audience recognizes. Use "Sound familiar?" framing to build connection.
  • Solution Introduction: Present your offer as the bridge from pain to dream outcome. Show the transformation, not just features.
  • Social Proof: Testimonials, case studies, logos, numbers. Place immediately after the solution section.
  • Value Stack: List everything included with real dollar values. Make the total value far exceed the price.
  • Inversion des risques: Garantie de remboursement, essai gratuit ou garantie de ROI. Éliminez toute raison de ne pas acheter.
  • Binary Close: Present two options - stay where you are vs. take action. Make inaction feel like the risky choice.
Key Rule: Every section should answer one question: "Why should I keep reading?" If a section doesn't earn the next scroll, cut it or rewrite it.

90-Day Marketing Planner

A structured quarterly planner that breaks your marketing goals into weekly actions. Includes KPI tracking sheets and campaign timelines.

Month 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

  • Define your ideal customer avatar and document it
  • Audit your current website and fix conversion leaks
  • Set up lead capture forms and automated follow-up
  • Launch your first email nurture sequence

Month 2: Traction (Weeks 5-8)

  • Start outbound outreach (cold email or social DMs)
  • Publish 2-3 pieces of content per week
  • Set up retargeting campaigns for website visitors
  • Collect your first 5-10 testimonials or reviews

Month 3: Scale (Weeks 9-12)

  • Double down on your highest-performing channel
  • Launch a referral or partner program
  • Automate your top 3 most repetitive workflows
  • Review KPIs and plan your next 90-day sprint
KPIs to Track Weekly: Leads generated, cost per lead, appointments booked, close rate, revenue per client, and client retention rate.

Automation Blueprint

The top 5 automations every small business should set up: lead capture, follow-up, booking, onboarding, and re-engagement.

Automation 1: Lead Capture

Form submission > Instant email confirmation > Add to CRM pipeline > Notify sales team via SMS > Tag based on source

Automation 2: Speed-to-Lead Follow-Up

New lead arrives > SMS sent within 60 seconds > Email with value content > If no reply in 2 hours, send follow-up > Assign to sales rep for manual outreach

Automation 3: Appointment Booking

Lead books a call > Calendar confirmation email > SMS reminder 24 hours before > SMS reminder 1 hour before > If no-show, trigger rebooking sequence

Automation 4: Client Onboarding

Deal marked "Won" > Welcome email sent > Onboarding questionnaire delivered > Access credentials created > Check-in email at Day 3, Day 7, Day 14

Automation 5: Re-Engagement

Contact inactive 30+ days > "We miss you" email > Special offer or content piece > If engaged, move back to active pipeline > If not, tag as cold for future campaigns
Impact: These 5 automations alone save most businesses 10-15 hours per week and prevent 40-60% of leads from falling through the cracks.

Client Onboarding Checklist

A step-by-step onboarding checklist to welcome new clients, set expectations, and deliver a premium experience from day one.

Before Day 1

  • Send a personalized welcome email within 1 hour of signing
  • Deliver an onboarding questionnaire to gather key details
  • Set up their account and provide login credentials
  • Schedule a kickoff call for Day 1-2

Week 1

  • Conduct the kickoff call - set expectations, timelines, and goals
  • Share a "Getting Started" guide or video walkthrough
  • Send a Day 3 check-in: "How's everything going?"

Week 2-4

  • Deliver on the first milestone or quick win
  • Week 2 check-in call to address questions
  • Request a testimonial or review after the first win
  • Introduce upsell or additional services naturally
Goal: Make the first 30 days feel premium. Clients who have a great onboarding experience are 3x more likely to stay long-term and refer others.

7-Figure Systems Playbook

How top-performing businesses structure their sales funnels, automations, and follow-up systems to scale past 7 figures.

Pillar 1: Lead Generation Machine

7-figure businesses don't rely on one channel. They run 3-5 lead sources simultaneously: paid ads, organic content, referral programs, cold outreach, and partnerships. Each source feeds into a single CRM with automated tagging and routing.

Pillar 2: Speed-to-Lead System

The #1 predictor of closing a deal is response time. Top businesses respond within 60 seconds via automated SMS, followed by a personal call within 5 minutes. Every lead gets at least 7 touchpoints in the first 48 hours.

Pillar 3: Conversion-Optimized Sales Process

A documented, repeatable sales process with clear stages, scripts, and objection handling. Every rep follows the same playbook. Close rates are tracked weekly and optimized monthly.

Pillar 4: Automated Fulfillment & Onboarding

Once a deal closes, zero manual work is needed to onboard the client. Welcome emails, questionnaires, access credentials, and check-in sequences all fire automatically.

Pillar 5: Retention & Expansion

The cheapest client to acquire is the one you already have. 7-figure businesses run systematic review collection, referral programs, and upsell campaigns that turn every client into a growth engine.
The Common Thread: Every pillar runs on systems, not people. The businesses that scale past 7 figures are the ones that automate everything that can be automated, so their team can focus on what only humans can do.
