Frequently Asked Questions

How does the commission structure work?

You earn a recurring commission on the monthly value of every client you refer — for as long as they remain a client. The more referrals you bring in, the higher your tier and the more you earn. Book a call with our team to get the full details.

Do I need to be an eNovo client to join?

It helps but isn't required. Being a client gives you first-hand experience to share authentic recommendations, but anyone with a network of business owners can apply.

How do I track my referrals?

Once approved, you get access to a partner dashboard where you can see clicks, sign-ups, active clients, and earnings in real time. You'll also receive monthly reports via email.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed monthly. You can receive payments via direct deposit (ACH) or PayPal. Once you hit the minimum payout threshold, your earnings are sent automatically.

Is there a cost to join?

The partner program is completely free to join. There are no fees, no minimums, and no obligations. Apply, get approved, and start earning.
