Questions

Pricing, Answered

Do all three plans include the same platform? +
Yes. Starter, Growth, and Scale all include the core eNovo CRM and automation tools. What changes between plans is capacity - users and contacts - and how much one-on-one help comes with it.
How does the 7-day free trial work? +
Every plan begins with a 7-day free trial so you can get into the platform and see how it fits your business before you commit. Pick the plan you want, start the trial, and explore.
What's included in the Growth onboarding sessions? +
Two 90-minute one-on-one sessions. We help configure the essentials, answer your questions, and tailor the setup to how your business actually operates - so you're not staring at an empty CRM wondering where to start.
What does the Customer Success Manager on Scale actually do? +
You're paired with a dedicated CSM and get one hour of one-on-one support each month - up to 12 hours a year. Use it to ask questions, troubleshoot, review your setup, and make sure the platform keeps up as you grow.
Can I change plans later? +
Yes. Start where you are today and move up as your team and contact list grow. If you're not sure which plan fits, book a call and we'll walk through it with you.
Is there a setup fee or a long-term contract? +
No setup fees. Monthly plans are billed month to month and you can cancel anytime. Annual plans are billed once for the year, and on Growth and Scale that works out to a month free compared to paying monthly.
