Part 3

Your First 5 Automations

Based on your score, here are the automations you should implement first, in priority order. Each one is designed to eliminate a specific bottleneck and start generating results within the first week.

Complete Part 1 above to get personalized automation recommendations based on your current stage.
1 Start Here

Instant Lead Response Sequence

When someone fills out a form, requests a quote, or sends a message, they automatically get an SMS + email within 60 seconds acknowledging their request and guiding them to book a call. Studies show that responding in under 5 minutes makes you 21x more likely to close. This automation alone replaces the need for a dedicated appointment setter for initial outreach.

Replaces: Manual follow-up, missed leads, slow response time
2 High Impact

No-Show & Cancellation Recovery

Sends automated reminders 24 hours, 1 hour, and 15 minutes before every booked call. If someone doesn't show, a recovery sequence kicks in: a "sorry we missed you" text, a rebooking link, and a follow-up email with social proof. Most businesses lose 20-30% of booked calls to no-shows. This cuts that number in half overnight.

Replaces: Manual reminders, lost appointments, wasted ad spend
3 Week 2

Long-Term Nurture Drip

Not everyone buys on the first touch. This 90-day drip sequence sends a mix of value emails, case studies, video testimonials, and soft CTAs to leads who went cold. It runs completely in the background and consistently resurfaces 10-15% of "dead" leads into booked calls each month, all without you lifting a finger.

Replaces: Forgetting about old leads, manual email blasts, missed revenue
4 Week 3

Review & Referral Engine

After every completed job or service delivery, this automation sends a personalized review request via SMS with a direct link to your Google Business profile. Happy clients are prompted to leave a review; unhappy ones are redirected to a private feedback form. A follow-up message asks for referrals with an easy shareable link. Businesses using this see 3-5x more reviews within the first month.

Replaces: Forgetting to ask for reviews, reputation management tools, manual follow-up
5 Month 2

Database Reactivation Campaign

Take every contact in your database who hasn't engaged in 90+ days and run a "we miss you" campaign. A strategic mix of SMS and email offers them a reason to come back: a free audit, a limited-time offer, or a simple check-in. Most businesses are sitting on hundreds or thousands of contacts they've already paid to acquire. This campaign typically generates $5,000-$20,000 in revenue from contacts you've already paid for.

Replaces: Wasted database, cold outreach, expensive new lead acquisition
