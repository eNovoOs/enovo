The Bottom Line: While HubSpot remains the "safe" corporate choice, its aggressive "per-contact" pricing model and fragmented Hub architecture are penalizing growing entrepreneurs. eNovo wins by providing a unified powerhouse that scales with your ambition, not your database size — eliminating the technical debt that typically requires a $5,000/month consultant.

The Pain Point: Paying for "Feature Bloat"

In today's digital economy, a CRM is your engine. But for many US entrepreneurs, HubSpot has evolved from a helpful tool into a "Golden Cage." We call it the "HubSpot Tax": you're lured in by the startup discount, only to be hit with massive price hikes as soon as you actually start succeeding and growing your lead list.

HubSpot segments its power into 5+ different "Hubs." For a scaling entrepreneur, this means paying for features you don't use just to unlock the one automation you actually need.

Do you really need enterprise-level multi-touch attribution if you're just trying to automate your sales funnel and keep your overhead low?
